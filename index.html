Join the club

Why join

There's a lot to love here.

A club that is genuinely for everyone, whatever first brought you to code.

Build things you're proud of

  • A fall project you pick yourself.
  • A spring group build with the whole club.
  • A beginner friendly hackathon.
  • A showcase to present your work.

Learn beyond class

  • Weekly sessions on what class skips over.
  • Talks from women in tech and LASA alumni.
  • Workshops you help choose.
  • Handpicked resources in the group chat.

Get a little something back

  • A points leaderboard for snacks, merch, and perks.
  • Real service hours for helping out.
  • Projects worth adding to a resume.
  • Lead roles to grow into.

Feel at home

  • Everyone is genuinely welcome.
  • You are never the only beginner.
  • Low pressure, lots of support.
  • Join in August or in February.

It fits around your life.

Come to what you can and build at your own pace. No pressure, and definitely no attendance police.

The year

How a year with us works.

Everyone leaves having built something, learned something, and made a few friends.

01
Fall

Your own project

Pick something you would love to make and build it at your own pace, with the club to help along the way.

02
Winter

The hackathon

One beginner friendly day, real teams, and a demo at the end. First timers are exactly who it is for.

03
Spring

The group build

The whole club builds one thing together, with a real role for everyone, whatever you like doing.

04
May

The showcase

Present your work to sponsors, mentors, and the school. A lovely thing to point to later, too.

What we get into

  • Where CS meets art, medicine, and music.
  • Staying sharp in a world full of AI.
  • The tools real developers use.
  • Friendly competitions, only if you want them.
